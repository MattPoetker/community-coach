October cohort: hot seats open Thursday, and six of you should take them

By Sara Kowalczyk · Two hours ago · 23 replies

Six slots, first come. Bring a live pricing problem — an actual proposal you have not sent yet, not a hypothetical, and not one you already lost. The difference matters more than people expect: a proposal you are still holding can be rewritten on the call, and one you have already sent can only be autopsied.

Last round we rewrote four scopes live and three of them closed inside a fortnight. The fourth is still open, at roughly double what its author originally intended to charge, which I am counting as the more instructive outcome of the two.

If you have never sat one, the format is unglamorous. You read your scope aloud. The room asks what the client actually buys. You discover, usually around the third question, that you have priced the deliverable rather than the decision it unblocks.
