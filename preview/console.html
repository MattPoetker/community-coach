Client wants to move from retainer back to project work

Nine months into a £3,800/month retainer. New procurement lead came in last month and wants everything itemised — deliverables, hours, unit rates, the lot.

My instinct is that itemising kills the whole arrangement, because the value is in being available and knowing their systems, and neither of those has a line item. But I also do not want to lose a client over a principle.

Do I hold the line, or take the project work and try to rebuild the retainer in six months once the new lead trusts me?
