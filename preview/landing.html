What actually changes when you stop selling days
◔ Private ◎ 1,284 members ◇ £89/month SK By Sara Kowalczyk

Momentum is for independent consultants who are good at the work and underpaid for it. Not a course you watch alone. A room of about twelve hundred people doing the same thing, and a set of live sessions where your actual proposals get taken apart.

What you get

  • Four courses, start to finish: positioning, pricing, proposals, retainers
  • Weekly office hours — open room, no agenda, bring the live problem
  • Monthly hot seats where six members rewrite a real scope on the call
  • Accountability pods of five, rebalanced each quarter
  • A searchable archive of every session since 2023
  • Proposal teardowns from members who have already priced past £100k

Who it is not for

  • Agencies with more than six people — the pricing work does not transfer
  • Anyone looking for lead generation; we do not teach that here
  • People who want templates rather than a rewrite of their own offer

Members typically move from day rates to a first retainer inside four to seven months. The median reported increase in effective hourly rate after a year is 62%. Both figures come from the annual member survey, and both are medians rather than best cases.

Fourteen days free, no card. If you join and it is wrong for you, cancel from your own settings — no email, no retention call.
